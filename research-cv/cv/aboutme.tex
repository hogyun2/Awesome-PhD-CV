\cvsection{Summary}

\begin{cvparagraph}
A Ph.D. student in the M.S.--Ph.D. integrated program at \href{https://sparolab.github.io/}{SPARO Lab, Inha University}, advised by \href{https://sites.google.com/site/ygchocv/home}{Prof. Younggun Cho}.
Researching scalable and collaborative autonomy for multi-robot systems: how robots perceive, map, and operate in shared environments through communication-efficient coordination.
Current focus is on communication-aware multi-robot SLAM and map merging, and on 3D foundation models for collaborative embodied agents.
